Our Story

Mzansi Memorabilia was founded in 2025 in Umhlanga, KwaZulu-Natal by Sizwe Ngcobo and Luvuyo Sigcau.

The idea was born from a shared passion for South African sport and a gap in the market — fans had no reliable, local destination for authentic sports memorabilia.

From signed jerseys to match-used equipment, every item we sell tells a story and connects fans to unforgettable moments in South African sporting history.

Our Mission

Mzansi Memorabilia aims to provide fans with authentic South African sports memorabilia, while celebrating and preserving the stories behind South Africa's rich sporting heritage.

Our Vision

Mzansi Memorabilia aims to become South Africa's number one destination for sports memorabilia, and to build a loyal, worldwide audience of South African sports fans.

What We Offer

  • Signed jerseys from legendary South African players across multiple sports
  • Match-used balls, boots, and other authenticated sporting equipment
  • Premium framed memorabilia, display-ready for your home or office
  • Limited edition collectibles available in very small quantities
  • Surprise collectible mystery boxes for the passionate fan
